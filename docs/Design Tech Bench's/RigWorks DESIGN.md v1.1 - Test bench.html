All values on this page come straight from DESIGN.md v1.1. Product names, prices and wattages are made up, and the power thresholds are for demonstration only. Use the dashed “Look for” notes to judge what calculation alone can't settle.

Colours

Surface ladder and text contrast

Each column is one surface, with every text tier on it. The ratios are calculated live in your browser from the token hex values.

Look for: can you see the step from canvas to surface-1 to surface-2 to surface-3 on your screen? The steps are 1.04–1.10:1, so on a dim laptop panel surface-2 and surface-3 may merge. That's acceptable only because borders and labels carry the meaning.

Accent roles

Links use primary-highlight #828fff. The same sentence with primary #5e6ad2 as text fails AA.

Semantic status on each surface

Look for: do the 14% tints read as status or as noise? Is amber clearly different from red for colour-blind viewers? Each badge also carries an icon and a word, so it should still work in greyscale. Use your browser's devtools rendering emulation to check.
Typography

Inter scale, Latin and Cyrillic

Tracking is in em, so the display sizes stay proportional when they shrink below 480px.

Look for: Cyrillic at 12–13px (caption, body-sm) in ink-subtle. Is it still comfortable? Does the negative tracking on Cyrillic display lines feel cramped compared with Latin?
